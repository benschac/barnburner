# Graphic assets

Downloaded November 9, 2026. Local copies keep the static recreation independent of runtime network access.

- `teams/*.svg`: Official NHL dark-background team logos, `https://assets.nhle.com/logos/nhl/svg/{TEAM}_dark.svg`. Includes all 32 team abbreviations, including `UTA`.
- `players/hughes.png`: Jack Hughes, `https://assets.nhle.com/mugs/nhl/20262027/NJD/8481559.png`.
- `players/allen.png`: Jake Allen, `https://assets.nhle.com/mugs/nhl/20262027/NJD/8474596.png`.
- `icons/home.svg`: Google Material Icons filled Home, `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/material-design-icons/master/src/action/home/materialicons/24px.svg`.
- `icons/flight.svg`: Google Material Icons filled Airplanemode Active, `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/material-design-icons/master/src/device/airplanemode_active/materialicons/24px.svg`.
- Material icon Apache 2.0 license: `icons/LICENSE`.
- `teams/WSH-alt.svg`: Official Washington Capitals secondary eagle logo, `https://assets.nhle.com/logos/nhl/svg/WSH_secondary_dark.svg`.
- `fonts/roboto-condensed.ttf`: Google Fonts variable Roboto Condensed, `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/robotocondensed/RobotoCondensed%5Bwght%5D.ttf`. License in `fonts/roboto-condensed-OFL.txt`.
- `fonts/graduate.ttf`: Google Fonts Graduate Regular, `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/graduate/Graduate-Regular.ttf`. License in `fonts/graduate-OFL.txt`. Its octagonal dotted zero and slab numeral details match the supplied graphic.
